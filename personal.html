More Than My Studies

Outside university, I am curious about the details that make everyday life interesting: music, spaces, food, movement and new places. These interests give me ways to stay creative and keep learning away from a screen.

Music

Music is one of the things I enjoy most in my free time. I love jazz and house music, as well as English indie music. I like how completely different styles can create different moods and become part of a person’s memories.

Design and Spaces

I am really interested in fashion and interior design. As a hobby, I look at furniture, houses and building interiors, paying attention to how colour, materials and layout change the feeling of a space.

Food and Balance

I have completed a course about food, nutrition and dietary choices. Since then, I have paid close attention to maintaining a balanced diet and making informed choices about what I eat.

Travelling

I have travelled throughout Spain and across Europe. I enjoy discovering cities, landscapes and local ways of life, often planning trips with BlaBlaCar and Skyscanner, which are honestly my best friends when I travel.

Staying Active

I enjoy running and am training for a marathon. I also like padel and weightlifting. Staying active helps me clear my head and gives structure to my week.
